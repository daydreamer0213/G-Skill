# Creator Profile

Use these as defaults, not restrictions. The current request wins when it differs.

## Positioning

- Identity: AI application practitioner and industry observer.
- Long-term goal: build a recognizable point of view and become a trusted opinion leader without pretending to know more than the evidence supports.
- Public identity: use an online name; no face on camera.
- Voice: reserved but willing to make clear judgments when evidence supports them.

## Content lines

Keep two independent lines:

1. Product and practice: the creator's products, AI tool tests, and real workflows.
2. Viewpoint and intelligence: AI news, technical evolution, industry interpretation, and personal judgment.

Current working mix is 50% AI tool tests, 30% AI news or new-feature briefings, and 20% AI workflow cases. Treat it as planning guidance rather than a quota.

Current projects: 按本次提供的项目资料。

## Production defaults

- About 10 hours per week.
- Multi-platform distribution.
- 16:9 horizontal master; create vertical content only when explicitly requested.
- No face; use real screen recording, product pages, source documents, self-made charts, voiceover, and large subtitles.
- Human voice or AI voice are both acceptable.
- Prefer a mature, dark, technology-oriented visual tone, but keep vertical layouts simple and readable.

## Established creative principles

- Product videos explain products; viewpoint videos express viewpoints.
- Start from what viewers recognize, then reveal the less visible mechanism.
- Let hotspots improve retention, maps or frameworks improve saves, and independent judgment earn follows.
- Explain how the human–AI relationship changes before diving into technical terms.
- Use popular products as era anchors, not as complete definitions of an era.
- Learn evidence, narrative momentum, and high-level synthesis from strong creators without copying their personal expression.
